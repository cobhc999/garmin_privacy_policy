# Privacy Policy – Activity+ GAP Gauge

Last updated: 2026-11-19

**Developer / contact:** T. Helfer, cobhc999@gmail.com

## What the app does with data
Activity+ GAP Gauge is a Garmin Connect IQ data field. While you record an activity it reads the speed, distance and altitude values your watch provides and calculates Grade Adjusted Pace on the watch.

## Data collection
- The app does **not** collect, transmit, store on any server, sell or share any personal data.
- The app has no internet or network access, no accounts and no analytics or advertising.
- All calculations run locally on your watch.

## Data written to your activity
With your watch's "FIT contributor" permission, the app adds two values (GAP and average GAP) to the activity file recorded by your watch. Your watch and Garmin Connect handle that file; this is governed by Garmin's privacy policy, not by the developer.

## Permissions
- **FIT contributor:** write GAP / average GAP into your activity file.
- **User profile:** read your unit preference (metric / statute) to display min/km or min/mi.

## Settings
Smoothing and fallback pace range are stored on your watch / in the Garmin Connect IQ app through Garmin's standard app settings mechanism.

## Payments
Purchases are processed by Garmin through the Connect IQ store. The developer receives no payment card details and no personal data about buyers beyond what Garmin includes in sales reports.

## Children
The app is a general-audience sports tool and is not directed to children under 13.
